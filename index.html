The only rule: match the seat letter and its colour on the insert to the holder — same letter, same colour, it fits.

Geometry, in plain words

A = slotting head (Horn calls it a driven broaching unit, for CNC). B = traditional broaching (Horn: conventional slotting). Seats H and I (16–20 mm) come in B only.

Keyway tolerance

Ground into the insert width: C11 loose · D10 / H9 sliding · JS9 / P9 standard DIN 6885 key fits. Pick the class on your drawing.

Remember to order

G1/8 coolant connector for IK holders is not included. Clamping screw & TORX PLUS wrench are listed as spare parts per holder. Grade AN45 · ap 0.02–0.15 mm per stroke.
